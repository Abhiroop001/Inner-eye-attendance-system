# Work Schedules, Shift Allocations, and Holiday Calendars Policy

## 1. Enterprise Work Schedules
1. **Standard General Schedule (`SCH-GEN-01`)**:
   - Timezone: `Asia/Kolkata` (IST, UTC+05:30) / `America/New_York` (EST, UTC-05:00)
   - Working Hours: 09:00 to 17:00
   - Grace Period: 15 minutes
   - Working Days: Monday through Friday (Weekly Off: Saturday & Sunday)
2. **Morning Operations Schedule (`SCH-OPS-01`)**:
   - Working Hours: 06:00 to 14:00
   - Grace Period: 10 minutes
   - Working Days: Monday through Friday
3. **Flexible Core Schedule (`SCH-FLEX-01`)**:
   - Working Hours: 10:00 to 18:00
   - Grace Period: 20 minutes
   - Core Presence Requirement: 11:00 to 16:00

## 2. Statutory & Company Holiday Rules
- The organization publishes a master `HolidayCalendar` covering all gazetted public holidays for the calendar year.
- Days marked in the official holiday calendar are automatically credited as `HOLIDAY` and are completely exempted from leave deduction calculations.
- Work performed on designated public holidays or weekly rest days is categorized as `OVERTIME_HOLIDAY` subject to 1.5x compensatory credit upon managerial approval.
